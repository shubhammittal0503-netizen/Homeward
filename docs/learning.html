Learning

The agent gets better from outcomes and from your corrections, and you can see and undo everything it has learned. The numbers on this page are synthetic history for the demo.

The loop, in one paragraph

Three signals feed it: outcomes (did the client pay within 7 days of a note; was a match confirmed or corrected; was a draft sent, edited or rejected), your edits, and your answers. They change only the choice within what the rules allow: which way to pay to offer first where the policy allows more than one, the wording of drafts, the lean on an ambiguous match, and payer names remembered once you confirm them. A learned preference is used after at least five outcomes of the same kind or one explicit confirmation from you. It never changes the clocks, the limits, the stop rules, the 0.80 threshold or which invoices need an export form. Policy section 11 is the rule.

Undo, and what happens after a wrong link

Every learned item has an undo here (in the demo the Undo buttons only explain; nothing is stored). If you approve a link and later correct it, the agent unlinks it before capture, or after capture prepares a correction note to Razorpay support and your bank for your approval; it demotes the payer name it learned from that match; and auto-approve switches off until 20 more matches are confirmed. The audit log keeps both entries; nothing is rewritten.

Earning more trust

The agent's last 31 matches were all confirmed as right when you approved them (synthetic). After 20, the agent offers to approve one low-risk step by itself: uploading an invoice that matches at 0.98 or above. It stays off until you switch it on in Controls, and everything it does is still logged.
