Not now snoozes this item until tomorrow morning's run. Deadlines still count down. Don't suggest this tells the agent not to suggest it again for 30 days. A no is a no.

Why now. Razorpay refunds a card payment that is not captured within 3 days. Oliver paid on Saturday 10 October at 10:42.
Limits that apply
  • Nothing goes to the client.
  • Only the invoice already in Zoho is uploaded.
Rules of the agent: Controls

What happened

  • Sat 10 Oct, 10:42 IST: Oliver Grant paid invoice SL-1051 (£1,800) by Mastercard. Status: authorised, waiting for an invoice. Razorpay payment record
  • Invoice SL-1051, UX research and design, £1,800, dated 7 Oct 2026, is in Zoho Books. Zoho Books
  • Razorpay captures international payments only with an invoice, and refunds card payments not captured within 3 days. Razorpay docs

How the agent decided

  1. Rule
    Exact match: same client, same amount, and the payment description says SL-1051.
  2. Rule
    Clock: 1 day left before the automatic refund (Tue 13 Oct, 10:42).
  3. Rule
    Invoice dated 7 Oct and you are an LLP, so it goes in October's export form, due 30 Nov. Razorpay's blog ties the EDF-number-before-capture rule to MoneySaver payments, so this card payment can be captured now.

What a fixed rule would do: A fixed rule also finds this one. What it lacks is the clock: nothing tells you it refunds tomorrow.
