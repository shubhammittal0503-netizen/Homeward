Playbook

Every situation the agent is built to handle: 49 of them, in four groups. For each one: how it is spotted, whether a rule or the AI decides, what the agent does, and what it never does. The demo walks through 12 of them. The first group is open; tap the others to open them.

Rules first, AI only for judgement. Deadlines, limits and exemptions are rules, because they must never be wrong. The AI reads what needs judgement: messy payer names and references, client replies in other languages, which way to pay, and drafts. Below 0.80 confidence, the agent asks you.
