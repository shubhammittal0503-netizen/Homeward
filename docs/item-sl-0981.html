Letter to ICICI Bank, trade desk
Request to extend the realisation period for export invoice SL-0981

Dear Sir or Madam,

Saffron Loop Studio LLP requests an extension of the period for realising the export value of invoice SL-0981 (USD 1,200, dated 20 January 2026, client Copperline Studios Pty Ltd, Australia).

Reason: the client has confirmed that the payment is scheduled in its November 2026 payment run (email of 30 September 2026, attached).

We request an extension to 31 December 2026.

Priya, Designated Partner, Saffron Loop Studio LLP

Approve and download letter

Not now snoozes this item until tomorrow morning's run. Deadlines still count down. Don't suggest this tells the agent not to suggest it again for 30 days. A no is a no.

Why now. Export money is due within nine months of the invoice date, and an exporter still unpaid a year past that can be restricted to advance payment only. SL-0981 is dated 20 January 2026, so the limit is 20 October 2026.
Limits that apply
  • You send the letter to ICICI; the agent does not file anything with the bank.
  • No message goes to Copperline.
Rules of the agent: Controls

What happened

  • Invoice SL-0981, pitch deck design, $1,200, dated 20 Jan 2026, is still unpaid. Zoho Books
  • On 30 Sep, Copperline's accounts team wrote that it is scheduled for their November payment run. Inbox
  • RBI: export proceeds are due within nine months; the bank can extend the time when the exporter asks with reasons. Unpaid a year past the due date, the exporter may only export against advance payment or a letter of credit. RBI FEMA 23(R)/2026-RB, Regulations 5 and 13
  • The invoice predates the 1 October export-form regime, so ICICI holds no declaration for it. The letter asks the bank to record the expected date, so the money can be received and matched without question when it arrives. Agent note

How the agent decided

  1. Rule
    Clock: 8 days to the nine-month limit.
  2. Rule
    Reminding Copperline to pay is the RazorpayX Receivables Agent's job, so Homeward does not chase them. Homeward handles the bank side.
  3. AI
    Drafted a request to ICICI to record an extension to 31 Dec 2026, using the reason in Copperline's email of 30 Sep.

What a fixed rule would do: A fixed rule can flag the date. It cannot find the reason in the client's email or write the request.
